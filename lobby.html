How to Learn Quotex Trading

1. What is Quotex?

Quotex is a binary options trading platform where you predict whether the price will move up (CALL) or down (PUT) in the next candle. If your prediction is correct, you earn 85% profit; if wrong, you lose the full investment. This is a high-risk game, so practicing on demo first is essential.

2. Understand the Platform Layout

The platform is divided into 5 main parts:

  • Top Header — UID, balance, and online users.
  • Asset Bar — Pairs like INR/USD, EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, AUD/USD.
  • Chart — 30-second candles, timer, and period number.
  • Trade Panel — Investment amount, CALL (green) and PUT (red) buttons.
  • Left Drawer — Deposit, Withdrawal, Transaction History, Logout.

3. What is a Candlestick?

Each candle represents a 30-second time period. It has 4 parts:

  • Open — Price when the period starts.
  • Close — Price when the period ends.
  • High — The highest price reached.
  • Low — The lowest price reached.

Green candle = close > open (price rose). Red candle = close < open (price fell). The bigger the body, the stronger the move. The longer the wick, the higher the volatility.

4. How Do You Place a Trade?

Follow these steps:

  • Step 1: Tap an asset chip to choose a pair.
  • Step 2: Enter the investment amount (start with ₹10).
  • Step 3: Watch the timer — betting locks in the last 10 seconds.
  • Step 4: If you think the price will rise, tap CALL (green); if it will fall, tap PUT (red).
  • Step 5: When 30 seconds end, the result appears — win gives +85% profit, loss takes the amount.

5. How to Practice on Demo

Start with only ₹10–₹50 per trade. Do 20–30 trades a day and track your win percentage. If you're above 55% win rate, you can move forward. But remember — this is demo; real markets are random.

6. Is Prediction Actually Possible?

The truth is no one can predict with 100% accuracy. Candle direction is random. Therefore:

  • Try to follow trends, but confirmation is difficult.
  • Never use martingale (doubling after a loss) — you can hit 5 losses in a row.
  • Place only one trade at a time, don't panic.
  • Set a stop loss — max 5 trades a day, max 3 losses.

7. The Biggest Mistakes

  • Emotional trading — placing a big bet after a loss out of anger.
  • All-in on one trade — sometimes a single trade wipes everything out.
  • Blind trust in tips or signals — no signal is 100% correct.
  • Trading with borrowed money — never do this.

8. The Path Forward

Spend your first 30 days on demo only. Note every trade. Learn the patterns that keep working. The Chart Patterns section has 15 classic patterns — study them; they will help in real life. But always remember: in trading, only practice, discipline, and risk management let you survive.

Trading Tips — 50 Rules

Money Management

1Risk only max 2% of your balance per trade.

2Set a daily target — 5% profit or 5% loss, then stop.

3Never use martingale (doubling after a loss).

4Only risk what you can afford to lose.

5Keep 60% of your capital in cash at the end of the day.

6Never trade with loan, EMI, or borrowed money.

7Do not exceed 10 trades per day.

8Withdraw 50% of weekly profit and keep it aside.

Entry Rules

9Only trade pairs you understand.

10After a big move, wait for a small pullback.

11Never place a bet during the last 10 seconds (locked time).

12Focus on just one asset at a time.

13Avoid trading around news events — volatility is high.

14After 3 consecutive losses, take a 30-min break.

15If you're bored, tired, or angry — stop trading.

Psychology

16Do not get overconfident during a win streak — the market can flip.

17Never chase losses — revenge trading is the most dangerous habit.

18Write down the reason for every trade — "why did I place this?" Review later.

19Limit screen time — no more than 2 hours.

20Keep your phone silent while trading — no distractions.

21After a loss, wait 5 minutes before the next trade.

22Happy on profit, sad on loss — both emotions ruin trading.

Chart Reading

23Small body + long wick = indecision — avoid.

24A Doji may lead to reversal, but confirm with the next candle.

25Hammer at the bottom = bullish hint. Shooting star at the top = bearish hint.

26After three consecutive green candles, a red one is more likely.

27Big body candles indicate a strong trend — follow them.

28A cluster of small candles = consolidation — wait for a breakout.

29Wick rejection zones matter — reversals often start there.

30Zoom into the chart — you'll miss small patterns otherwise.

Beginner Mistakes

31Skipping demo and going straight to real money.

32Blindly trusting YouTube tips.

33Joining signal groups and losing money.

34Doing 100 trades a day.

35Obsessing over covering every loss.

36Taking random entries with no plan.

37Believing "guaranteed profit" bots.

38Treating trading like gambling.

Pro Habits

39Trade only in a fixed time slot each day.

40Maintain a weekly journal — track your win rate.

41Stick to one strategy for 30 days; don't change every 5 minutes.

42Celebrate small wins; don't increase risk chasing big ones.

43Review your chart for 10 minutes after trading.

44Take care of your health — sleep, food, exercise.

45After every trade, ask: "Should I have taken this?"

46Stay disciplined even on boring days.

47Write your rules and stick them near your screen.

48Don't over-trade even on good days.

49Trade only with your own money, never borrowed.

50Most importantly: surviving is the biggest profit of all.

Remember: No tip is 100% guaranteed. This is general guidance only. Trade at your own risk.
